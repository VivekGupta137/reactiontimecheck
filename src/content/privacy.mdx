# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** February 12, 2026  
**Last Updated:** February 12, 2026

At CheckReactionTime, we are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring that your personal information is handled with care. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, and safeguard your data when you use our website and services.

## Our Privacy-First Approach

CheckReactionTime is designed with privacy at its core. We believe in providing valuable tools without compromising your personal data or tracking your behavior across the web.

**Key Privacy Principles:**
- ✅ **No account registration required** - Use our tools anonymously
- ✅ **Local data storage** - Your test results stay on your device
- ✅ **No personal data collection** - We don't ask for or store personal information
- ✅ **No third-party tracking** - No social media pixels or advertising trackers
- ✅ **Transparent operations** - Clear about what data we handle

## Information We Collect

### Data We DO NOT Collect
- Personal identifiers (names, emails, addresses)
- User account information
- Personal demographics
- Device fingerprinting
- Location data
- IP addresses for tracking purposes
- Social media profiles or connections

### Data Stored Locally on Your Device
The following data is stored exclusively in your browser's local storage and never transmitted to our servers:

**Reaction Time Test Results:**
- Test session results (reaction times, accuracy, timestamps)
- Performance analytics and metrics
- Historical data for trend analysis
- Test configuration settings (rounds, delay settings, mouse click preferences)

**Number Memory Test Results:**
- Game state and progress
- Session settings and preferences
- Performance history

**User Preferences:**
- Display settings
- Test configuration options
- Analytics viewing preferences

This data remains on your device and is only accessible to you. You can clear this data at any time through your browser settings.

### Minimal Technical Data
We may collect minimal, non-identifying technical information solely for improving service reliability:

- Basic page performance metrics
- Error reporting for debugging
- General usage statistics (anonymized)

This technical data cannot be linked to individual users and is used only for service improvement.

## How We Use Information

### Local Data Usage
Data stored on your device is used to:
- Display your test results and performance analytics
- Track your progress over time
- Remember your preferred settings
- Provide personalized insights and recommendations

### Technical Data Usage
Any minimal technical data we collect is used to:
- Improve website performance and reliability
- Fix bugs and technical issues
- Understand general usage patterns to enhance user experience
- Ensure cross-browser compatibility

## Data Storage and Security

### Local Storage
- **Location:** Your browser's local storage on your device
- **Control:** You have complete control over this data
- **Deletion:** You can clear this data anytime via browser settings
- **Persistence:** Data persists until you clear it or uninstall your browser

### Technical Security Measures
- HTTPS encryption for all communications
- Secure hosting infrastructure
- Regular security updates and monitoring
- No sensitive data transmission to external servers

## Third-Party Services

We do not use:
- Google Analytics or similar tracking services
- Social media tracking pixels
- Advertising networks
- Third-party data collection tools
- Marketing automation platforms

Our website is designed to function independently without relying on external tracking or data collection services.

## Your Rights and Choices

### Data Control
- **Access:** Review all your data through the analytics dashboard
- **Deletion:** Clear your data anytime through browser settings
- **Export:** Download your data using built-in export features
- **Settings:** Adjust preferences for data collection and display

### Browser Settings
You can control data storage by:
- Disabling local storage in browser settings
- Using incognito/private browsing mode
- Clearing browser data regularly
- Adjusting cookie and storage preferences

## Children's Privacy

CheckReactionTime does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Since we don't collect personal data from any users, our service is inherently safe for children. However, we recommend parental supervision for all online activities.

For technically minded users, here's how we implement privacy:
