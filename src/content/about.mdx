# About CheckReactionTime

CheckReactionTime is a minimalist platform built to help you measure, train, and improve your reaction time, reflex speed, and cognitive performance.

We create fast, accurate, and distraction-free online tools used by gamers, athletes, students, and professionals around the world.

## Our Mission

Our mission is simple: to build high-quality performance testing tools that are free, accurate, and enjoyable to use.

In a world full of noisy apps and distractions, we focus on minimal design, precise measurement, and meaningful feedback — so you can track real improvement over time.

Whether you're training for competitive gaming, improving your athletic performance, or simply curious about your cognitive abilities, our tools provide the data you need to measure and monitor your progress.

## What We Build

At CheckReactionTime, we build online tools and games that help measure and improve:

- **Reaction time and reflex speed** – Visual stimulus testing with millisecond precision
- **Focus and attention** – Track your alertness and mental sharpness
- **Cognitive performance** – Monitor brain processing speed over time
- **Hand-eye coordination** – Essential for gaming and sports performance

Our most popular tool is the **Reaction Time Test**, designed to work smoothly on desktop, tablet, and mobile devices. It features comprehensive analytics, progress tracking, and performance comparisons to help you understand and improve your reflexes.

## Accuracy & Methodology

We design our tests to be simple, fast, and scientifically grounded.

Reaction time is measured in milliseconds based on the exact moment you respond to a visual stimulus. We minimize delays by using:

- **Lightweight code** – Optimized for minimal processing overhead
- **High-precision timers** – JavaScript performance APIs for accurate timing
- **Optimized rendering** – Smooth visual transitions without lag
- **Local data storage** – Your results stay on your device for privacy

Please note that device latency, screen refresh rate, and browser performance may slightly affect results. Factors like input lag, display response time, and system performance can add 10-30ms to measurements. For most accurate results, use a device with a high refresh rate display (120Hz+) and wired peripherals.

**Our tools are intended for training and self-measurement, not for medical or diagnostic use.**
